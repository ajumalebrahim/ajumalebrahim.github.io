CASE STUDY / TALABAT

Improving how a mobile team ships.

My focus included CI/CD automation and reducing mobile maintenance effort, alongside order tracking, testing, and payment integrations.

The engineering challenge

Improve the delivery workflow through CI/CD automation and reduce the development effort needed to maintain mobile features.

My contribution

I worked on CI/CD automation and helped move native order tracking to Flutter with a BFF approach. I also built testing frameworks with developers and integrated payment providers including Apple Pay and Checkout.com.

The reported outcome

The reported 40% maintenance-effort reduction reflects the lower number of developers needed after the Flutter migration. Features also shipped faster. Separately, coverage improved from 0% to 70% within the testing initiative.
